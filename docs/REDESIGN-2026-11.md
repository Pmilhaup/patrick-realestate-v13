## Second pass (17 Nov, evening): trophy sales, film, interactive data

**Trophy sales.** `src/data/trophy.json` holds the tier ($2,500,000), each sale's rank by price among MLS closed single-family sales in its village and year, and the story pages' facts and photographs. `src/pages/sold/[slug].astro` renders one page per story. The homepage rail, the Properties trophy grid and the record chart all read `listings.json` joined to this file. To add a story: prepare photographs under `public/media/sold/<name>/` (2000 and 1000 px WebP, plus `hero-*`), add them to `media.json`, add the story to `trophy.json`, and set `href` in `listing-display.json`.

**Film.** The homepage opens on drone footage (`public/media/home/film-1080.mp4`, `film-portrait-720.mp4`; a 16-second loop cut from `DJI_0650.MP4`, 12 Feb 2024). The poster is the film's first frame, so the change from still to film is invisible. The film is requested only after the page has loaded, never with reduced motion or data saver, and stops off screen and on Pause.

**Charts.** `src/components/Viz.astro` + `src/scripts/viz.js` (Apache ECharts, tree-shaken, loaded when a figure nears the viewport):

| kind | What it shows | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `history` | Monthly record since 2008 with a 12-month trend, carried to today on nightly data | Home, six village guides |
| `motion` | Supply against median price for seven villages, month by month since 2008 | Intelligence |
| `seasons` | Month-by-year heat map with the average year above it | Selling |
| `record` | Every published sale by year and price | Home, Properties |

**Data.** `public/data/history.json` is baked by `tools/sync_history.py` from the public view `mv_market_intelligence` (monthly rows through February 2026). Re-run it when a new settled month is loaded. The nightly tail comes from `daily_snapshots` in the browser.

**Map.** `tools/build_shore.mjs` projects the seven villages' Census boundaries with D3 Geo into `src/data/shore.json`; `CoastMap.astro` draws them and `site.js` shades them by the latest nightly figure.

**Open data question: months of supply.** The nightly table's `months_supply` and `homes_for_sale` do not match the monthly record. Lake Forest, October 2026: 46 homes for sale and 2.4 months in the monthly record; 113 homes and 8.1 months in the nightly table on 16 November. Closed sales and prices agree at the seam; inventory does not. Until that is reconciled the site shows days on market instead of supply wherever the figure is nightly, and the `history` chart does not extend supply past the monthly record.

**Page transitions.** Chrome sometimes skips the cross-page photographic transition (observed in headless test runs, more often while large images are decoding). The page then simply appears. The layout suppresses the resulting unhandled-rejection message.

**Performance and accessibility pass (commits `1ccf647`, `3e54a16`).**

- Intelligence: the embedded market map is `<iframe data-src="/market-map">`; `site.js` sets `src` when the frame is within 300 px of the viewport. Loading it with the page cost about 20 s of main-thread time on a simulated phone.
- Motion chart: moving marks sit on their own canvas layer (`zlevel: 1`), pixel ratio is capped at 1.5, and it autoplays once only on a wide screen with a fine pointer. On phones and under reduced motion it opens on the latest month and plays on request.
- Legacy pages (`body.is-legacy`): `--ink-4`, `--ink-5` and `--fg-fade` are lifted to `#8492AD` (5.6:1 on navy). The older style sheets set them at 4.1:1.
- Masthead link and property tiles carry visible text as their accessible name; no `aria-label` overrides on links.

Measured on the deployed preview, Lighthouse mobile: Home LCP 1.6 s (performance 99); `/sold/28-aberdeen-court` 1.5 s (100); Properties 2.2 s (99); Intelligence 1.7 s, blocking time 1.2 s (75). Accessibility 100 on all four. Intelligence's remaining blocking time is ECharts parse plus the page's own older dashboard script.

## Third pass (17 Nov, late): more stories, a budget tool, the age of the houses

**Four more sold stories.** `/sold/1300-w-kennicott-drive`, `/sold/611-oak-knoll-drive`, `/sold/530-oak-knoll-drive`, `/sold/1339-lawrence-avenue`. Same template and data file (`src/data/trophy.json`). They sit below the trophy tier, so they appear under "Sold" on Properties and are not in the "More trophy sales" strip. Facts are from the MLS record; photographs are the photographer's delivered files, not the edited copies:

| Listing | Source folder used | Left out, and why |
|---|---|---|
| 1300 W. Kennicott | `MRED Photos` | |
| 611 Oak Knoll | `VHT Photos - Large Raw` | Family room: framed portraits on the wall |
| 530 Oak Knoll | `Original Photos - Media` | Living room: wall of family photographs. Great room cropped to drop a shelf of framed photographs. `Final` and `Edited` are upscaled or edited copies |
| 1339 Lawrence | `VHT Photos/MRED` | Seven files in `Final - MLS Photos` are 1536 x 1024 AI edits, including the lead exterior and the primary bedroom. Furnished living, dining and family rooms left out until staging is confirmed |

Not built: 1350 W. Whitmore (the folder holds the 1330 house photographs, none of the land) and 417 E. Deerpath (walk-through snapshots only, no marketing photographs).

The rank line now reads "The top N percent" down to the top third and "Above the village median" below that. The median label sits a row under "This sale" so the two cannot collide on a phone.

**Budget tool (Buying).** `kind="budget"`: a slider and six presets move one budget across seven villages. Each village is drawn from its closed-sale quantiles (5th to 95th percentile, middle half, median) on a log price scale. A table under the chart gives, for each village: share of sales at or below the budget, sales within 15 percent of it, and the median size, lot, year built and days to contract of those sales, plus how many closed at or above asking. Medians show only where at least five sold. The table is a real `<table>` and restacks on a phone.

**Age of the houses (Neighborhoods).** `kind="eras"`: homes by decade built, stacked by village or one village at a time; a second view shows what each decade's homes have sold for in the past 36 months (a village-by-decade grid for all seven, bars for one village). The sentence under the chart is computed from the same figures.

**Data for both.** `public/data/budget.json` and `public/data/eras.json`, aggregates only (counts and medians, no individual sale). `raw_sales` is not public, so the path is: run `tools/sql/budget.sql` and `tools/sql/eras.sql`, save the rows as `tools/data/*_raw.json`, run `python tools/build_market_extras.py`. Each row carries a checksum computed in the database and the script refuses to write if a row does not add up. A public aggregate view in Supabase would let these refresh nightly like `history.json`; that is a database change and has not been made.

**Loading.** The chart engine is imported only after the page's `load` event, so it never competes with the hero image.
